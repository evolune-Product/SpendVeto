How to put spending limits on x402 agent payments

Write the limits into a SpendVeto policy — per-call cap, hourly budget, call rate, approval threshold, allowed chains and payees — and pay through SpendVeto's SDK, MCP server or proxy. Policy is checked before anything is signed, and the payment gate re-checks it server-side, so an agent with its own key and a hand-rolled client still can't overspend.

The policy

{
  "$schema": "https://spendveto.com/schemas/policy-v1.json",
  "maxPerCallUSD": 0.25,
  "maxPerHourUSD": 2,
  "maxCallsPerHour": 60,
  "requireApprovalAboveUSD": 0.10,
  "allowedChains": ["base", "solana-devnet"],
  "allowedPayees": ["0xVendorA…", "vendor.eth"],
  "categoryCapsUSD": { "data": 1 },
  "anomaly": { "burstAttempts": 20, "burstWindowSeconds": 10 }
}

What is enforced, and where

  • Before signing: the agent's own pipeline runs the full check (freeze, OFAC sanctions screening, chain and payee allowlists, trading hours, caps, delegation scopes) and asks a human above the approval line, failing closed on timeout.
  • At the payment gate: the server re-runs the same policy and consumes the single-use approval, so a client that skips its own check is still refused.
  • Holds: an open upto ceiling or auth-capture escrow counts against the hourly budget until it settles.
  • Proof for the seller: the payment carries a governed-spend attestation.
  • Around the gate: governed wallets are reconciled against their on-chain USDC transfers; a payment SpendVeto never authorized counts against the same budget and delegation caps, and can freeze the wallet.

How this differs from wallet-level limits

Wallet providers offer allowances on their own wallets. Coinbase's Spend Permissions grant an allowance per period, and its Agentic Wallets cap per session and per transaction. SpendVeto's policy is wallet-agnostic and covers 20 chains across eight signature families. It adds human approvals, payee allowlists with ENS names, delegated budgets that cascade, and the same budget for the agent's model and card spend.

Questions

How do I limit an AI agent's x402 payments?

Give it a policy (per-call cap, hourly budget, approval threshold, allowlists) and pay through a governed path that checks the policy before signing and again at the payment gate.

Which chains are supported?

Twenty registered chains across eight signature families; on-chain settlement is live on six testnets through x402's public facilitator, and the rest settle locally until a facilitator supports them.

What if the agent holds its own private key?

The payment gate re-runs the policy server-side and requires a real, single-use approval above the threshold, so a client that skips its own checks is still refused.

How to accept payments only from governed AI agents

Require a governed-spend attestation: a five-minute token, signed by the buyer's SpendVeto only after its policy allowed the payment, naming the payer, payee, resource and ceiling. Add requireGovernedAgent({ trustedIssuers, audience, price }) in front of a paid route and it verifies the token offline. It makes no call to SpendVeto, and every check fails closed.

The middleware

import { requireGovernedAgent } from "spendveto/integrations/seller-express.js";

app.get("/api/report",
  requireGovernedAgent({
    trustedIssuers: ["0xBuyerIssuer…"],   // buyers' SpendVeto issuer addresses you accept
    audience: "0xYourPayTo…",             // your payout address(es)
    price: 0.25,                          // USD, or (req) => price
  }),
  (req, res) => res.json(buildReport(req.spendveto)));

What gets refused, with its code

SituationCode
No attestationattestation_missing
Signed by an issuer you don't trust (including a self-minted one)attestation_untrusted_issuer
Altered after signingattestation_bad_signature
For another payee, resource or a lower priceattestation_audience_mismatch · attestation_resource_mismatch · attestation_amount_insufficient
Issued to a different wallet than the one payingattestation_payer_mismatch
Expired, or presented a second timeattestation_expired · attestation_replayed

The middleware refuses to start with an empty trust list. Anyone can run an issuer with a permissive policy, so there is no "accept any issuer" mode.

How this relates to AP2 mandates and Mastercard Verifiable Intent

Google's AP2 signs what a user authorized, with price and timing limits. Mastercard's Verifiable Intent ties together who the user is, what they instructed and what happened. A SpendVeto attestation answers a different question: did the paying organization's policy engine (budgets, approvals, payee rules, kill switch) allow this specific payment on this rail? These are separate claims and they compose: a payment can carry a user mandate and an organization attestation side by side.

What it proves, and what it doesn't. It proves the named issuer's engine said yes to this payer, payee, resource and ceiling at that time. It doesn't prove the payment happened, or that the issuer's policy is strict. You learn the policy's version, never the buyer's limits. Whether an issuer is trustworthy is your call about that issuer.

Questions

How can a merchant verify an AI agent's payment was authorized?

Ask for a signed statement from the buyer's side and verify it against a key you trust. With SpendVeto, the buyer's policy engine signs a governed-spend attestation after it allows the payment, and the seller verifies it offline with one middleware.

Do I need to run SpendVeto to verify attestations?

No. Verification is a secp256k1 signature check (EIP-191) against the issuer address; it is a standard EIP-191 signature recovery, so any Ethereum library can do the same.

Is this the same as AP2 or Verifiable Intent?

No — those sign what a user authorized; a SpendVeto attestation signs what the paying organization's policy decided. They answer different questions and can travel together.

How to control what an AI agent can buy with a virtual card

Point Stripe Issuing's real-time authorization webhook (or Lithic's Auth Stream Access) at SpendVeto and every swipe on an agent's card is decided by policy: a per-authorization cap, a rolling daily budget, hourly velocity, merchant-category blocks, the kill switch and merchant reputation — answered in the provider's own format.

What it adds to the issuer's own controls

Stripe Issuing's spending controls block merchant categories, countries or merchants, and limit spend per authorization or per month. SpendVeto decides each authorization against rolling daily budgets and hourly velocity, the agent's shared budget across its model, x402 and payout spend, a kill switch, and merchant reputation shared across companies (card-merchant:<network id>). An authorization is answered within the issuer's window. A merchant flagged for review is declined, because the swipe can't wait for a human.

Honest limits. SpendVeto decides authorizations; captures, reversals and refunds are not consumed yet, and an approved authorization is booked as committed spend.

Questions

Can I give an AI agent a virtual card safely?

Yes, if every authorization is decided by policy in real time: caps, a rolling budget, velocity, merchant-category blocks and a kill switch. Stripe Issuing and Lithic both let a webhook approve or decline each swipe.

What does the agent's card do when the budget is used up?

The next authorization is declined in the issuer's own format, with the SpendVeto code in the decision metadata.

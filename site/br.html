What's real, with sources

Nighttime limit ("horário noturno"): every Pix-enabled account defaults to a BRL 1,000 ceiling on transfers made during the customer's chosen nighttime window (20:00–06:00 by default, or 22:00–06:00) — raising it requires a minimum 24-hour cooling-off period the institution must enforce.

Sources: Tecnoblog — BC nighttime window rules

Pix Automático (BCB's recurring-Pix scheme, mandatory for payer-side institutions since October 2025): the payer authorises once, in their own banking app; a business then pulls against it under BCB's own rules — each charge created 2–10 days before its due date, at most 3 charge attempts (including dunning retries) in any rolling 7-day window. Cancellation takes effect only once BCB confirms it.

Sources: Juspay — Pix Automático rules

EU agent payments, governed — against PSD2's own exemption math.

An AI agent cannot supply Strong Customer Authentication — it is, by definition, something the payer knows, has, or is. SpendVeto decides whether an agent's SEPA Direct Debit mandate debit may proceed, using the exact exemption mechanics the RTS itself defines, and pauses for the payer whenever none of them apply.

What's real, with sources

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2018/389 — the RTS on Strong Customer Authentication under PSD2. Three exemptions, each scoped exactly as the regulation defines it: Art. 10 — a one-off remote payment skips SCA up to €30 per transaction, €100 cumulative since the last SCA, or 5 consecutive transactions. Art. 14(1) — a recurring series of the SAME FIXED amount to the SAME payee needs SCA only once; a changed amount voids the exemption. Art. 18 (TRA) — a PSP with a low enough reference fraud rate may waive SCA up to €100 / €250 / €500, depending on its own declared rate.

Sources: EUR-Lex — Regulation (EU) 2018/389 · Freenance 2026 SCA/TRA guide

SEPA Direct Debit Core Rulebook (European Payments Council): the creditor must notify the payer at least 14 calendar days before each collection, unless a shorter period is agreed. The payer has an 8-week no-questions-asked refund right on an authorised debit (13 months if unauthorised).

Sources: European Payments Council — SDD Core Rulebook
